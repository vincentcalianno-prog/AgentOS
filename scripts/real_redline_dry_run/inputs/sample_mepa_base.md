# MASTER ENERGY PURCHASE AGREEMENT
## Generic Template — Dry-Run Harness Fixture

**Agreement Reference:** MEPA-TEMPLATE-001  
**Contract Type:** master-energy-purchase-agreement  
**Version:** Outbound (Operator Position)

---

## ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

### 1.1 Defined Terms
"Agreement" means this Master Energy Purchase Agreement including all schedules and exhibits attached hereto.

### 1.2 Counterparty
"Counterparty" means the entity executing this Agreement as the purchasing party.

### 1.3 Delivery Point
"Delivery Point" means the physical location(s) specified in the applicable Transaction Confirmation where energy is transferred.

### 1.4 Energy Product
"Energy Product" means electricity, renewable energy certificates, or associated attributes as specified in each Transaction Confirmation.

### 1.5 Transaction Confirmation
"Transaction Confirmation" means a written document executed by both parties specifying the terms of an individual energy transaction.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — PURCHASE AND SALE OBLIGATIONS

### 2.1 Purchase Obligation
Operator agrees to sell and deliver, and Counterparty agrees to purchase and receive, the Energy Product in quantities specified in each Transaction Confirmation.

### 2.2 Transaction Confirmations
Each Transaction Confirmation shall be executed in writing and shall incorporate the terms of this Agreement. In the event of conflict, this Agreement shall control except where the Transaction Confirmation expressly overrides a specific provision.

### 2.3 Delivery Obligations
Operator shall deliver the Energy Product to the Delivery Point at the times and quantities specified in the applicable Transaction Confirmation, subject to Force Majeure and Curtailment rights set forth herein.

### 2.4 Acceptance Obligation
Counterparty shall accept and pay for all Energy Product delivered to the Delivery Point in accordance with the applicable Transaction Confirmation.

### 2.5 Scheduling
Each party shall perform its respective scheduling obligations under applicable tariffs and agreements, and shall cooperate in good faith to fulfill scheduling requirements.

### 2.6 Curtailment Rights
Operator retains the right to curtail delivery of Energy Product due to operational constraints, grid operator instructions, or Force Majeure events, provided that Operator provides written notice to Counterparty as soon as practicable.

### 2.7 Ramp Rate
Operator shall deliver Energy Product at ramp rates consistent with applicable grid interconnection standards and shall not be required to exceed such rates.

### 2.8 Measurement
Energy quantities shall be measured using Operator's revenue-grade metering equipment, which shall be tested and calibrated in accordance with applicable ANSI standards at least once per calendar year.

### 2.9 Liability Cap
Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall be limited to direct damages only. In no event shall either party be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, punitive, or exemplary damages, regardless of whether such damages are based on contract, tort, strict liability, or any other theory.

### 2.10 Change in Law
If any change in applicable law materially affects a party's ability to perform its obligations under this Agreement, the affected party shall provide written notice within thirty (30) days of becoming aware of such change, and the parties shall negotiate in good faith to amend this Agreement.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — PRICING AND PAYMENT

### 3.1 Contract Price
The Contract Price for each Energy Product transaction shall be as specified in the applicable Transaction Confirmation, expressed in USD per MWh.

### 3.2 Payment Terms
Counterparty shall pay all invoices within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Invoices not paid within such period shall accrue interest at the lesser of (a) one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or (b) the maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

### 3.3 Invoice Disputes
Counterparty may dispute any invoice amount in good faith by providing written notice to Operator within fifteen (15) days of receipt of the invoice, specifying the basis for the dispute. Undisputed amounts shall be paid when due.

### 3.4 Netting
The parties agree to net all amounts owed to each other under this Agreement in the same billing period, and only the net amount shall be payable.

### 3.5 Taxes
Each party shall be responsible for all taxes, assessments, and governmental charges imposed on such party with respect to the Energy Product, with Counterparty bearing all taxes after the Delivery Point.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — TERM AND TERMINATION

### 4.1 Agreement Term
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force and effect until terminated in accordance with the provisions hereof.

### 4.2 Transaction Term
Each Transaction Confirmation shall specify its own term, which may not extend beyond the termination of this Agreement.

### 4.3 Termination for Default
Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other party commits a Material Default and fails to cure such default within thirty (30) days of written notice thereof.

### 4.4 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason upon sixty (60) days prior written notice to the other party, provided that all Transaction Confirmations in effect shall continue to be performed for their stated terms unless separately terminated.

### 4.5 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, all outstanding payment obligations shall survive termination. Each party shall promptly settle all amounts owed under Transaction Confirmations that were in effect at the time of termination.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

### 5.1 Mutual Representations
Each party represents and warrants that: (a) it is duly organized and validly existing; (b) it has full authority to execute and perform this Agreement; (c) execution does not violate any law, regulation, or agreement to which it is a party.

### 5.2 Operator Representations
Operator represents and warrants that it holds, or will hold at the time of delivery, all licenses, permits, and authorizations required to sell and deliver the Energy Product.

### 5.3 Counterparty Representations
Counterparty represents and warrants that it holds all licenses, permits, and authorizations required to purchase and receive the Energy Product at the Delivery Point.

### 5.4 No Reliance
Each party acknowledges that it has not relied on any representation, warranty, or statement of the other party not expressly set forth in this Agreement.

---

## ARTICLE 6 — INDEMNIFICATION

### 6.1 Mutual Indemnification
Each party (the "Indemnifying Party") shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other party and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all third-party claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to the Indemnifying Party's breach of this Agreement or gross negligence or willful misconduct.

### 6.2 Indemnification Procedure
The party seeking indemnification shall: (a) promptly notify the Indemnifying Party in writing of any claim; (b) grant the Indemnifying Party sole control of the defense; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation and assistance at the Indemnifying Party's expense.

### 6.3 Limitation on Indemnification
The indemnification obligations in Section 6.1 shall not apply to any loss or damage to the extent caused by the Indemnified Party's own negligence or willful misconduct.

---

## ARTICLE 7 — FORCE MAJEURE

### 7.1 Force Majeure Events
Neither party shall be liable for failure or delay in performance of its obligations under this Agreement (other than payment obligations) to the extent such failure or delay is caused by a Force Majeure Event.

### 7.2 Definition of Force Majeure
"Force Majeure Event" means any event beyond the reasonable control of the affected party, including but not limited to: acts of God, fire, flood, earthquake, storm, lightning, epidemic, war, terrorism, acts of governmental authority, grid operator curtailment orders, or failure of third-party transmission facilities.

### 7.3 Notice and Mitigation
The party claiming Force Majeure shall provide written notice to the other party within five (5) business days of the occurrence and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the effects of and overcome the Force Majeure Event.

---

## ARTICLE 8 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### 8.1 Good Faith Negotiation
The parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute arising under or related to this Agreement through good faith negotiation between senior representatives of each party within thirty (30) days of written notice of the dispute.

### 8.2 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of law principles.

### 8.3 Venue
Any legal proceeding arising under this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Travis County, Texas.

---

## ARTICLE 9 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 9.1 Entire Agreement
This Agreement, including all exhibits and Transaction Confirmations, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, and understandings.

### 9.2 Amendments
This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

### 9.3 Assignment
Neither party may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

### 9.4 Notices
All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed delivered when: (a) delivered by hand; (b) sent by overnight courier; or (c) sent by email with confirmation of receipt.

### 9.5 Waiver
Failure to exercise any right or remedy under this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of such right or remedy.

### 9.6 Severability
If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
